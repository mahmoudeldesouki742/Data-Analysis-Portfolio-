## 🚀 Featured Projects

### 1. Favorita Grocery Sales Forecasting

**Focus:** Exploratory Data Analysis | Time Series Analysis | Sales Forecasting | Business Intelligence

An end-to-end data analysis project based on grocery store sales data, focusing on understanding historical sales patterns, identifying business trends, and developing forecasting insights.

**Project Highlights:**
- Data exploration and preprocessing.
- Handling missing values and data quality issues.
- Analyzing sales performance across stores and product families.
- Identifying temporal patterns, trends, and seasonality.
- Investigating external factors affecting sales.
- Applying forecasting techniques to understand and predict future sales behavior.
- Presenting analytical findings through visual reports.

**Project Contents:**
- **Main Notebook:** Data preprocessing, analysis, and forecasting workflow.
- **Report:** Visual reports presenting key findings and analytical insights.

**Technologies:** Python, Polars, DuckDB, Power BI.

### 2. Retail Store Sales Dirty for Data Cleaning

**Focus:** Data Cleaning | Data Preprocessing | Exploratory Data Analysis | Power BI

A practical project focused on working with messy retail sales data and addressing data quality issues before transforming it into a reliable dataset suitable for analysis and reporting.

The project demonstrates the importance of data quality and the role of systematic cleaning and preprocessing in producing accurate analytical results.

**Project Highlights:**
- Inspecting raw datasets and identifying data quality issues.
- Detecting and handling missing values.
- Identifying and treating duplicate records.
- Correcting inconsistent values and data types.
- Cleaning and preparing datasets for analysis.
- Building interactive Power BI reports to explore the cleaned data.

**Project Contents:**
- **Python Notebook:** Data inspection, cleaning, and preprocessing workflow.
- **Power BI Reports:** Interactive dashboards and visual reports.

**Technologies:** Python, Pandas, Power BI.
